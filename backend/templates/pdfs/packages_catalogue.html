01  /  How to read this catalogue

Three numbers tell the whole story.

Each family on the following pages is a starting point — final specifications come out of a free site survey and a load profile we build with you. The three indicators below are the language we'll use throughout the document.

Inverter
kVA
The peak instantaneous load the system can handle without tripping.
Battery
kWh
Stored energy. Drives how many hours you stay on through an outage.
Solar PV
Panels
Daily generation. Sized to recharge the battery faster than you discharge.
A note on variants
Inside each family, variants differ in panel count and battery capacity — the inverter is the same. So 'Home Luxury 1.0' and 'Home Luxury Performance' run on the same inverter, but Performance ships with more panels and a meatier battery for longer autonomy.
