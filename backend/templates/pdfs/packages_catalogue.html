01  /  How to read this catalogue

Three numbers tell the whole story.

Each family on the following pages is a starting point — final specifications come out of a free site survey and a load profile we build with you. The three indicators below are the language we'll use throughout the document.

Inverter
kVA
The peak instantaneous load the system can handle without tripping. Decides which appliances can run together.
Battery
kWh
Stored energy. Directly drives how many hours you stay on through an outage at your normal nightly load.
Solar PV
Panels
Daily generation. Sized to recharge the battery faster than you discharge it on a sunny day.
A note on variants
Inside each family, variants differ in panel count and battery capacity — the inverter is the same. So 'Home Luxury 1.0' and 'Home Luxury Performance' run on the same inverter, but Performance ships with more panels and a meatier battery for longer autonomy. Pricing follows specification; a real quote comes after a site survey.
