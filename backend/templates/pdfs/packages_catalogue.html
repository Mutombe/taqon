01  /  How to read this catalogue

Sized for the way you actually live and work.

Each family on the following pages is a starting point — final specifications come out of a free site survey and a load profile we build with you. The kVA figure tells you the inverter class; battery and panel counts scale within each family to match how many hours of back-up you need.

kVA
Inverter class — the peak instantaneous load the system can handle without tripping.
kWh
Battery storage — directly drives how many hours you stay on through an outage.
Panels
Solar PV — sized to recharge the battery faster than you discharge it on a normal day.
